Prepare a separation checklist for: $ARGUMENTS

Use `references/hiring-firing.md` and `references/wage-hour.md` in the `ca-employment-rules` skill, and the `hr-practice` skill. Use roles and placeholders, not names.

Return:
1. **Risk check (before deciding)** - recent protected activity (complaints, leave, accommodation requests, safety reports, workers' comp), protected characteristics and timing, consistency with how others were treated, documentation quality, and whether an automated decision system was involved (human review and notice required from July 1, 2027). Recommend an employment attorney if any flag is present.
2. **Final pay** - what's due (wages, accrued vacation/PTO, commissions, expense reimbursement) and when, based on termination vs. resignation with or without notice; waiting time penalty exposure.
3. **Documents to provide** - EDD DE 2320 pamphlet, written notice of change in status, COBRA/Cal-COBRA, and any required benefits or retirement notices.
4. **Meeting plan** - who attends, a short factual script, return of property, system access, and final paycheck handoff.
5. **If a severance agreement is offered** - note ADEA/OWBPA timing for employees 40+, California limits on confidentiality and non-disparagement, and that counsel should draft it.
6. **Mass layoff check** - whether Cal-WARN or federal WARN could be triggered.
