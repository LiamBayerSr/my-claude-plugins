Review this handbook or policy for California compliance: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `ca-employment-rules` skill (especially `new-laws-2026-2027.md`) and the `hr-practice` skill.

If no document was attached or pasted, ask for it. Ask for headcount and work locations if not given, or state assumptions.

Return:
1. **Must fix** - provisions that conflict with current law or are missing required content (for example, sick leave below 40 hours/5 days, "use it or lose it" vacation, non-compete language, missing designated-person bereavement starting 2027). For each: the section, the problem, the law, and suggested replacement language.
2. **Should update** - outdated references, unclear language, and 2027 changes to get ahead of.
3. **Consider** - best-practice improvements and at-will or consistency risks.
4. **Missing policies** - required or strongly recommended policies not found (for example, workplace violence prevention plan reference, lactation, reproductive loss leave).

Quote the handbook only briefly to identify each section. End with a short summary count and recommend attorney review before republishing.
