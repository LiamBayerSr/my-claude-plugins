Work through this leave question: $ARGUMENTS

Use `references/leave.md` in the `ca-employment-rules` skill and the privacy guidance in the `hr-practice` skill. Do not ask for medical details; only what's needed to determine eligibility.

If headcount, the employee's length of service and hours, or the reason category is missing and changes the answer, ask in one short message or state assumptions.

Return:
1. **Laws that may apply** - for example, CFRA, PDL, FMLA (50+), paid sick leave, bereavement, reproductive loss leave, disability accommodation under FEHA, local ordinances - with eligibility for each given the facts.
2. **How much leave and protection** - job protection, benefits continuation, and whether leaves run concurrently.
3. **Pay** - which leave is paid by the employer, what accrued time can be used, and state wage-replacement benefits (SDI, PFL) the employee can apply for.
4. **Next steps for HR** - notices to give and deadlines, certification to request, the interactive process if accommodation is involved, and return-to-work steps.
5. **Risk flags** - for example, termination or discipline during or after leave; recommend counsel where applicable.
