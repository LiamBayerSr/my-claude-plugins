Build an onboarding checklist for: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `ca-employment-rules` skill (`hiring-firing.md`, `leave.md`, `training-notices.md`, `pay-rates.md`) and the `hr-practice` skill.

Organize by timing:
1. **Before the offer** - pay transparency, Fair Chance Act steps, background checks.
2. **Offer letter** - required and recommended contents; confirm pay meets the minimum wage or exempt threshold for the start date's year and location.
3. **Day one** - required notices and pamphlets, Form I-9 (within three business days), W-4 and DE 4, Wage Theft Prevention Act notice for non-exempt hires, Know Your Rights notice and emergency contact option, policies and acknowledgments.
4. **First 30-90 days** - benefits enrollment windows, sick leave eligibility, harassment prevention training deadline (within 6 months), workplace violence prevention training.
5. **Ongoing** - annual notices and training cycles.

Mark items as **Required** or **Recommended**, cite the law for required items, and flag local requirements to check for the work location.
