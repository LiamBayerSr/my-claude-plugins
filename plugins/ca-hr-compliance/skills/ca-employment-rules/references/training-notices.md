# Training, plans, reporting, and postings (California)

Verified November 2026 against the Labor Code, Government Code, and DIR and Civil Rights Department guidance.

## Sexual harassment prevention training (Gov. Code §12950.1) - 5+ employees
- Supervisors: 2 hours; non-supervisory employees: 1 hour.
- Within 6 months of hire or promotion to supervisor, then every 2 years. Seasonal and temporary employees have shorter deadlines.
- Must include abusive conduct and harassment based on gender identity, gender expression, and sexual orientation, with interactive elements.
- **Starting January 1, 2028 (AB 1803):** anti-hate speech content must be added.

## Workplace Violence Prevention Plan (Labor Code §6401.9, SB 553) - most employers
- Since July 1, 2024: a written, site-specific plan; a violent incident log; annual interactive training; and records retention. Exemptions are narrow (for example, places with fewer than 10 employees not accessible to the public, and remote workers).
- **Starting January 1, 2027 (AB 1961):** employers may seek workplace violence restraining orders protecting the workforce at a location, not just a named employee.

## Pay data reporting (Gov. Code §12999) - 100+ employees, or 100+ workers through labor contractors
- Annual report to the Civil Rights Department, typically due in May.
- **Starting 2027:** reports use 23 Standard Occupational Classification (SOC) major groups instead of the 10 EEO-1 categories (SB 464), and the penalty for repeated failure to file rises to up to $1,000 per employee (SB 1237).

## Annual and ongoing notices
- **Workplace Know Your Rights notice (SB 294):** standalone written notice to each employee annually (first due February 1, 2026) and at hire.
- **Required postings:** federal and California posters (minimum wage, paid sick leave, CFRA/PDL, harassment, whistleblower, Cal/OSHA, workers' comp, and others), plus local minimum wage and sick leave posters where applicable. Post electronically for remote workers.

## Surveillance and data (new for 2027)
- **AB 1883:** no AI-based workplace surveillance that infers employees' emotional states or collects neural data.
- **AB 1331:** no monitoring in bathrooms and similar private areas.
- **CCPA:** employee and applicant data are covered; SB 923 expands deletion rights to data held by vendors, and automated decision-making technology rules require compliance by January 1, 2027.
