# New California employment laws, 2026 and 2027

Verified November 2026 from employer-side law firm summaries of bills signed in the 2025 and 2026 legislative sessions. Confirm bill text at leginfo.legislature.ca.gov before relying on details.

## Effective in 2027

## Effective in 2028
- **AB 1803:** anti-hate speech content added to harassment prevention training (Jan 1, 2028).
- **SB 590:** Paid Family Leave benefits for care of a designated person (July 1, 2028).

## Took effect in 2026 (still new to many employers)
- **SB 294:** Workplace Know Your Rights notice and emergency contact designation.
- **SB 642:** pay transparency (good-faith pay range; broader definition of wages).
- **AB 692:** stay-or-pay ban for agreements on or after Jan 1, 2026.
- **AB 406:** leave and sick leave use for crime victims and witnesses in court proceedings.
- **SB 648:** Labor Commissioner enforcement of tip protections.
- **SB 513:** personnel records include education and training records.
- **SB 617:** Cal-WARN notice content on workforce services.
- **SB 303:** bias-mitigation training protections.
- **FEHA automated-decision regulations:** effective October 1, 2025; employers can be liable for discriminatory outcomes from AI hiring tools, including vendors'.
