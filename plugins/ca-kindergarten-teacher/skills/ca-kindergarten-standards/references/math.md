# Kindergarten Math (California Common Core) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

Major emphases for the year: counting and cardinality, and understanding addition and subtraction within 10 with objects and drawings.

## Counting & Cardinality (K.CC)
- **K.CC.1** Count to 100 by ones and by tens.
- **K.CC.2** Count forward from any given number (not only from 1).
- **K.CC.3** Write numbers 0-20; represent a number of objects with a written numeral 0-20.
- **K.CC.4** Understand counting and cardinality: say number names in order pairing one object to one number; the last number said tells how many; each successive number is one more.
- **K.CC.5** Count to answer "how many?" for up to 20 objects in a line, array, or circle, or up to 10 scattered; given a number 1-20, count out that many objects.
- **K.CC.6** Identify whether a group of objects is greater than, less than, or equal to another group (up to 10), by matching and counting.
- **K.CC.7** Compare two numerals between 1 and 10.

## Operations & Algebraic Thinking (K.OA)
- **K.OA.1** Represent addition and subtraction with objects, fingers, drawings, sounds, acting out situations, verbal explanations, expressions, or equations.
- **K.OA.2** Solve addition and subtraction word problems within 10 using objects or drawings.
- **K.OA.3** Decompose numbers 10 or less into pairs in more than one way (5 = 2 + 3 and 5 = 4 + 1).
- **K.OA.4** For any number 1-9, find the number that makes 10 when added to it.
- **K.OA.5** Fluently add and subtract within 5.

## Number & Operations in Base Ten (K.NBT)
- **K.NBT.1** Compose and decompose numbers 11-19 into ten ones and some further ones, using objects or drawings (18 = 10 + 8).

## Measurement & Data (K.MD)
- **K.MD.1** Describe measurable attributes of objects, such as length or weight.
- **K.MD.2** Directly compare two objects with a common attribute and describe the difference (taller/shorter, heavier/lighter).
- **K.MD.3** Classify objects into given categories, count the number in each, and sort the categories by count (up to 10).
- **K.MD.4** (California addition) Show understanding of concepts of time (morning, afternoon, evening, today, yesterday, tomorrow, week, year) and tools that measure time (clock, calendar).

## Geometry (K.G)
- **K.G.1** Describe objects in the environment using names of shapes and positional words (above, below, beside, in front of, behind, next to).
- **K.G.2** Correctly name shapes regardless of orientation or size.
- **K.G.3** Identify shapes as two-dimensional (flat) or three-dimensional (solid).
- **K.G.4** Analyze and compare 2D and 3D shapes using informal language (sides, corners, equal lengths).
- **K.G.5** Model shapes by building them (sticks, clay) and drawing them.
- **K.G.6** Compose simple shapes to form larger shapes.

## Standards for Mathematical Practice
MP1 make sense of problems and persevere · MP2 reason abstractly and quantitatively · MP3 construct arguments and critique reasoning · MP4 model with mathematics · MP5 use tools strategically · MP6 attend to precision · MP7 look for structure · MP8 look for regularity in repeated reasoning
