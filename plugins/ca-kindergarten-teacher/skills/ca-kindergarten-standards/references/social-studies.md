# Kindergarten History-Social Science (California) - paraphrased

Theme: **Learning and Working Now and Long Ago.** Students learn about their classroom and school community, and begin comparing the present with the past.

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

- **K.1** Understand that being a good citizen involves acting in certain ways: following rules (sharing, taking turns), learning examples of honesty, courage, determination, and responsibility from stories and real people, and understanding the importance of fair play and good sportsmanship.
- **K.2** Recognize national and state symbols and icons, such as the national and state flags, the bald eagle, and the Statue of Liberty.
- **K.3** Match simple descriptions of work people do with the names of related jobs at school, in the local community, and from historical accounts.
- **K.4** Compare and contrast the locations of people, places, and environments: determine relative location (near/far, left/right, behind/in front), distinguish land and water on maps and globes, build simple maps of the classroom or neighborhood, and identify traffic symbols and map symbols.
- **K.5** Put events in temporal order using a calendar, placing days, weeks, and months in proper order.
- **K.6** Understand that history relates to events, people, and places of other times: identify the purposes of and people honored in commemorative holidays, know the triumphs in American legends and historical accounts through stories, and compare daily life long ago with today (transportation, school, work, dress).

## Teaching notes
- Keep it concrete and connected to students' own lives: classroom rules, school helpers, family routines.
- Holiday-related content should explain who is honored and why, without assuming which holidays families celebrate.
