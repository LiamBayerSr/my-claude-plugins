Adapt this kindergarten lesson or activity: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `kindergarten-teaching` skill (especially English learner support and student privacy) and the `ca-kindergarten-standards` skill (ELD proficiency levels).

If nothing was pasted or described, ask the teacher to paste the activity. Describe student needs, never names.

Return:
1. **The core goal** - the one standard the adaptation must protect. Every version keeps the same goal.
2. **English learners** - concrete changes for Emerging, Expanding, and Bridging, each with a sentence frame.
3. **Students who need more support** - for reading, writing, or math: chunking, visuals, manipulatives, decodable text, read-aloud, fewer items, or partner work.
4. **Students ready for more** - deeper, not more of the same: open-ended problems, explaining reasoning, creating their own examples.
5. **Accommodations menu** - quick options a teacher could apply for students with IEPs or 504 plans (extra time, reduced copy work, alternate response mode), noting that the plan itself always governs.

Keep each section short and ready to use tomorrow.
