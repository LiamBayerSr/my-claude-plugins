## Credits and other individual items
- Earned income tax credit maximum: $685 (no children), $4,573 (1 child), $7,552 (2 children), $8,496 (3+ children). Investment income limit: $12,600. Phase-out ranges are in Rev. Proc. 2026-35, section 3.06.
- Child tax credit: $2,300 per qualifying child; refundable portion up to $1,800.
- Adoption credit: up to $18,250, of which up to $5,280 may be refundable. Phase-out between $273,680 and $313,680 MAGI.
- Lifetime Learning Credit phase-out (not indexed): $80,000-$90,000 MAGI single; $160,000-$180,000 joint.
- Personal exemption: $0.
- Itemized deductions: the overall (Pease) limitation stays eliminated, but taxpayers in the 37% bracket still face a cap on the tax benefit of itemized deductions.
- SALT deduction cap: $40,804 ($20,402 married filing separately), reduced above $510,050 of income ($255,025 MFS), not below $10,000 (OBBBA 1% annual increase; figures from the 2027 draft Form W-4, which says "total income" - verify MAGI mechanics in final Schedule A instructions). Rev. Proc. 2026-35 does not state a 2027 amount.
- Foreign earned income exclusion: $137,300.

## Estate and gift
- Basic exclusion amount: $15,480,000 per decedent dying in 2027.
- Annual gift exclusion: $20,000 per recipient; $201,000 for gifts to a non-citizen spouse.

**Traditional IRA deduction phase-outs (MAGI)**
- Single / head of household, covered by workplace plan: $83,000-$93,000
- Married filing jointly, contributor covered: $133,000-$153,000
- Married filing jointly, contributor not covered but spouse covered: $250,000-$260,000
- Married filing separately, covered: $0-$10,000 (not indexed)

**Roth IRA contribution phase-outs (MAGI)**
- Single / head of household: $158,000-$173,000
- Married filing jointly: $250,000-$260,000
- Married filing separately: $0-$10,000 (not indexed)

**Saver's credit AGI limits:** $83,000 joint; $62,250 head of household; $41,500 single and married filing separately. Starting in 2027 the Saver's Match (section 6433) replaces the Saver's credit for retirement plan and IRA contributions; the credit continues only for ABLE account contributions (Notice 2026-66, footnote 2).
