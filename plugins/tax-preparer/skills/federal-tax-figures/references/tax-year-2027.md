## Standard deduction
| Filing status | Amount |
|---|---|
| Single / Married filing separately | $16,600 |
| Married filing jointly / Surviving spouse | $33,200 |
| Head of household | $24,925 |

Additional standard deduction for age 65+ or blind: $1,700, or $2,100 if unmarried and not a surviving spouse (Rev. Proc. 2026-35). The separate $6,000 senior deduction for 2025-2028 also still applies (see the OBBBA file).

## Alternative minimum tax
| | Exemption | Phase-out begins |
|---|---|---|
| Unmarried | $93,000 | $515,950 |
| Married filing jointly | $144,700 | $1,031,900 |

28% AMT rate applies above $252,500 ($126,250 married filing separately).

## Credits and other individual items
- Earned income tax credit maximum: $685 (no children), $4,573 (1 child), $7,552 (2 children), $8,496 (3+ children). Investment income limit: $12,600. Phase-out ranges are in Rev. Proc. 2026-35, section 3.06.
- Child tax credit: $2,300 per qualifying child; refundable portion up to $1,800.
- Adoption credit: up to $18,250, of which up to $5,280 may be refundable. Phase-out between $273,680 and $313,680 MAGI.
- Lifetime Learning Credit phase-out (not indexed): $80,000-$90,000 MAGI single; $160,000-$180,000 joint.
- Personal exemption: $0.
- Itemized deductions: the overall (Pease) limitation stays eliminated, but taxpayers in the 37% bracket still face a cap on the tax benefit of itemized deductions.
- SALT deduction cap: Not yet released by the IRS as of November 9, 2026; look up before use. Rev. Proc. 2026-35 does not state a 2027 amount.
- Foreign earned income exclusion: $137,300.

## Estate and gift
- Basic exclusion amount: $15,480,000 per decedent dying in 2027.
- Annual gift exclusion: $20,000 per recipient; $201,000 for gifts to a non-citizen spouse.
