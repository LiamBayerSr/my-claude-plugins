# Tax year 2026 (returns filed in 2027)

Verified November 2026. Primary source: IRS newsroom release on tax year 2026 inflation adjustments and Revenue Procedure 2025-32 (https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf). Retirement figures: IRS 2026 plan limits release (November 2025).

## Standard deduction
| Filing status | Amount |
|---|---|
| Single / Married filing separately | $16,100 |
| Married filing jointly / Surviving spouse | $32,200 |
| Head of household | $24,150 |

Additional standard deduction for age 65+ or blind still applies (see Rev. Proc. 2025-32 for amounts), plus the separate $6,000 senior deduction for 2025-2028 (see OBBBA file).

## Credits and other individual items
- Earned income tax credit maximum (3+ qualifying children): $8,231. Other categories: Rev. Proc. 2025-32.
- Child tax credit: $2,200 per qualifying child (OBBBA; inflation-indexed). Refundable portion and phase-outs: verify in Rev. Proc. 2025-32 and Form 8812 instructions.
- Adoption credit: up to $17,670, of which up to $5,120 may be refundable.
- Lifetime Learning Credit phase-out (not indexed): $80,000-$90,000 MAGI single; $160,000-$180,000 joint.
- Personal exemption: $0.
- Itemized deductions: the overall (Pease) limitation is eliminated, but taxpayers in the 37% bracket face a cap on the tax benefit of itemized deductions.
- SALT deduction cap: $40,400 for 2026, reduced for MAGI above $505,000, not below $10,000 (OBBBA; verify phase-out mechanics in Schedule A instructions).
- Foreign earned income exclusion: $132,900.

## Estate and gift
- Basic exclusion amount: $15,000,000 per decedent dying in 2026 (OBBBA made the higher exclusion permanent, indexed after 2026).
- Annual gift exclusion: $19,000 per recipient; $194,000 for gifts to a non-citizen spouse.
