# Recurring federal deadlines (individuals and small businesses)

When a date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day. Disaster declarations can postpone deadlines for affected areas; check IRS disaster relief announcements for the client's county.

| Deadline | What's due |
|---|---|
| January 15 | Q4 estimated tax payment for the prior year (Form 1040-ES) |
| January 31 | W-2s to employees and SSA; 1099-NEC to recipients and IRS; most 1099-MISC to recipients |
| February 15 | Brokerage Forms 1099-B and consolidated statements (typical) |
| March 15 | Partnership (Form 1065) and S corporation (Form 1120-S) returns, or extension (Form 7004); K-1s to partners and shareholders |
| April 15 | Individual returns (Form 1040) or extension (Form 4868); C corporation returns (calendar year); Q1 estimated tax; prior-year IRA and HSA contributions; FBAR (FinCEN 114, automatic extension to October 15) |
| June 15 | Q2 estimated tax; returns for U.S. citizens living abroad (automatic 2-month extension) |
| September 15 | Q3 estimated tax; extended partnership and S corporation returns |
| October 15 | Extended individual returns; extended FBAR |

An extension to file is not an extension to pay. Interest and failure-to-pay penalties run from the original due date.

For any specific year, confirm dates on IRS.gov, since holidays and disaster relief shift them.
