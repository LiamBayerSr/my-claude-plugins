# One Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-21, signed July 4, 2025) - individual provisions

The IRS also refers to these provisions as the "Working Families Tax Cuts." Several new deductions are claimed on Schedule 1-A and are available whether or not the taxpayer itemizes. Source: IRS newsroom guidance on the new deductions and Schedule 1-A (https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/one-big-beautiful-bill-act-tax-deductions-for-working-americans-and-seniors). Verify details against current form instructions; IRS guidance on these provisions continued to develop after enactment.

## Temporary deductions, tax years 2025-2028

**Qualified tips**
- Up to $25,000 per year; self-employed can't deduct more than net income from the business that earned the tips.
- Phase-out begins above $150,000 MAGI ($300,000 joint).
- Not available to self-employed individuals in a specified service trade or business (SSTB) or to employees of SSTB employers.
- Requires SSN; married taxpayers must file jointly.

**Qualified overtime compensation**
- Up to $12,500 per year ($25,000 joint), limited to the FLSA-required premium portion (the "half" in time-and-a-half).
- Phase-out begins above $150,000 MAGI ($300,000 joint).
- Requires SSN; married taxpayers must file jointly.

**Qualified passenger vehicle loan interest**
- Up to $10,000 per year.
- Phase-out begins above $100,000 MAGI ($200,000 joint).
- Loan originated after December 31, 2024, secured by a lien, for a new vehicle for personal use; used vehicles and leases don't qualify. Refinanced amounts generally qualify.
- Car, minivan, van, SUV, pickup, or motorcycle under 14,000 lbs GVWR with final assembly in the U.S. VIN required on the return each year.

**Enhanced senior deduction**
- $6,000 per eligible individual age 65+ by year-end ($12,000 if both spouses qualify), in addition to the existing additional standard deduction for age 65+.
- Phase-out begins above $75,000 MAGI ($150,000 joint).
- Requires SSN; married taxpayers must file jointly.

## Other major individual changes
- **Tax rates and brackets:** the TCJA rate structure (10% through 37%) was made permanent.
- **Standard deduction:** the higher TCJA-level standard deduction was made permanent and increased, then indexed.
- **Child tax credit:** increased to $2,200 per child starting 2025 and indexed for inflation; SSN requirements apply.
- **SALT cap:** raised from $10,000 to $40,000 for 2025, increasing 1% per year through 2029 ($40,400 for 2026), with a reduction for high-MAGI taxpayers but not below $10,000. Reverts to $10,000 in 2030 under current law.
- **Estate and gift exclusion:** $15,000,000 for 2026, made permanent and indexed after 2026.
- **QBI deduction (Section 199A):** made permanent.
- **Itemized deductions:** the Pease limitation is gone, replaced by a limit on the benefit of itemized deductions for taxpayers in the 37% bracket.

When advising on any of these, state the effective years and recommend confirming with the latest IRS instructions, since guidance has been issued in stages.
