Research this federal tax question: $ARGUMENTS

If the tax year or a fact that changes the answer is missing, state your assumption at the top, or ask one short question if the answer would swing entirely on it.

Return a memo:
1. **Question** - restated precisely, with the tax year.
2. **Short answer** - two or three sentences.
3. **Facts and assumptions** - what the answer depends on.
4. **Analysis** - the rule, its application to these facts, and exceptions or limits. Cite IRC sections, regulations, and IRS guidance inline.
5. **Confidence and risk** - settled, likely, or uncertain; note any substantial-authority or disclosure considerations.
6. **Open questions** - facts to confirm with the client.
7. **Sources** - list of authorities cited, with IRS.gov links where available.

Verify any figure or rule that may have changed recently (especially 2025 law changes) against IRS.gov before relying on it.
