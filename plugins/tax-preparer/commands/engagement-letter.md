Draft an engagement letter for this service: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `tax-practice-standards` skill. If the practitioner shares their firm's existing letter or template, follow its structure and language instead of the outline below.

Default outline:
1. Parties and the specific services covered (tax years, forms, entities).
2. Services not covered (audit defense, state returns, bookkeeping) unless listed.
3. Client responsibilities: complete and accurate information, records retention, and deadlines for providing documents.
4. Firm responsibilities and the limits of the engagement (no audit or verification of client data; the firm relies on information provided).
5. Fees and billing, using placeholders.
6. Confidentiality, including §7216 consent language if any return information will be used or disclosed beyond return preparation.
7. Electronic communication and secure document exchange.
8. Term, termination, and records return.
9. Signature blocks.

Note at the top that the firm should have the letter reviewed against its professional liability carrier's or professional organization's requirements before use.
