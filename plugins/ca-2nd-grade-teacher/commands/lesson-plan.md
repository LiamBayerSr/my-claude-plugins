Draft a California 2nd-grade lesson plan for: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `second-grade-teaching` skill for lesson design and the `ca-grade2-standards` skill to choose and cite standards.

If no topic or standard was given, ask for one in a single short question. If no length was given, assume 45 minutes.

Include, in this order:
1. **Standards** - primary code(s) with a one-line paraphrase; supporting standards; Math Practice or CA NGSS practice if relevant; ELD focus.
2. **Learning target** - "I can..." in kid language, and a success criterion the teacher can observe.
3. **Materials** - common classroom items only.
4. **Vocabulary** - 3-5 words with kid-friendly definitions.
5. **Lesson sequence** - hook, I do, we do, you do, with minutes for each. Write the key teacher think-aloud lines.
6. **Check for understanding** - an exit ticket with an answer key.
7. **Differentiation** - support, extension, and ELD supports with one sentence frame each for Emerging, Expanding, and Bridging.
8. **Family connection** - one optional at-home activity, low-cost and doable in any home.

Keep it to what fits on about two printed pages. End by offering a matching worksheet (`/worksheet`) or rubric (`/rubric`).
