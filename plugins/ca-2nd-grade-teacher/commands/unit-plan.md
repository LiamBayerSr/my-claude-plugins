Plan a 2nd-grade unit on: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `ca-grade2-standards` skill for alignment and the `second-grade-teaching` skill for design. Default to 3 weeks if no length was given. If the teacher names a district curriculum or pacing guide, fit the unit to it.

Provide:
1. **Big idea and essential question** in kid language.
2. **Standards** - priority standards, supporting standards, and cross-subject links (for example, a science unit that also hits informational reading and opinion writing).
3. **End-of-unit assessment** - a performance task students can actually do, plus how it will be scored.
4. **Lesson-by-lesson map** - a table with day, learning target, key activity, and quick check. Mark lessons where small-group reteaching fits.
5. **ELD throughline** - the academic language students will build across the unit, with frames.
6. **Materials and texts** - suggest read-alouds by title and author only if you are confident they exist; otherwise describe the kind of text to look for.
7. **Family letter** - a short note introducing the unit.

Keep the map to one table the teacher can paste into a planner.
