# California English Language Development (ELD) Standards - grade 2 overview

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/el/er/eldstandards.asp

California's ELD standards describe how English learners use English to engage with academic content. They are used together with the content standards, not in place of them.

## Two kinds of ELD time
- **Integrated ELD:** every content teacher supports English learners during math, science, social studies, and ELA, using the ELD standards alongside the content standards.
- **Designated ELD:** a protected time during the day for targeted instruction based on students' English proficiency levels.

## Structure
- **Part I - Interacting in Meaningful Ways**
  - Collaborative: exchanging information and ideas, interacting via written English, supporting opinions, adapting language choices
  - Interpretive: listening actively, reading closely, evaluating language choices, analyzing language choices
  - Productive: presenting, writing, justifying and arguing, selecting language resources
- **Part II - Learning About How English Works:** structuring cohesive texts, expanding and enriching ideas (verbs, noun phrases, modifiers), connecting and condensing ideas.
- **Part III - Using Foundational Literacy Skills:** apply foundational reading skills, with attention to the student's home language, prior literacy, and similarities and differences with English.

## Proficiency levels
| Level | Students can... | Typical supports |
|---|---|---|
| **Emerging** | use English for immediate needs; learning basic everyday and some academic words | substantial support: visuals, gestures, realia, sentence frames with word banks, home language |
| **Expanding** | use English in a growing range of academic contexts | moderate support: sentence starters, graphic organizers, structured partner talk |
| **Bridging** | use English to engage in grade-level academic tasks | light support: precision of vocabulary, complex sentence work, extended academic talk |

## Planning moves
- Name the academic language demand of each lesson (e.g., "compare using 'longer than' / 'shorter than'").
- Write one sentence frame per level for the key task.
- Build in structured talk (turn-and-talk, think-pair-share) before writing.
- Pre-teach 3-5 tier-two and content vocabulary words with visuals.
