# Grade 2 English Language Arts (California Common Core) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

## Reading: Literature (RL.2)
- **RL.2.1** Ask and answer who, what, where, when, why, and how questions about key details.
- **RL.2.2** Retell stories, including fables and folktales from diverse cultures; determine the central message, lesson, or moral.
- **RL.2.3** Describe how characters respond to major events and challenges.
- **RL.2.4** Describe how words and phrases (rhythm, rhyme, alliteration, repeated lines) supply meaning in a story, poem, or song.
- **RL.2.5** Describe a story's overall structure: how the beginning introduces it and the ending concludes it.
- **RL.2.6** Recognize differences in characters' points of view, including using different voices when reading dialogue aloud.
- **RL.2.7** Use illustrations and words to understand characters, setting, or plot.
- **RL.2.9** Compare and contrast two or more versions of the same story by different authors or from different cultures.
- **RL.2.10** Read and comprehend grade 2-3 literature with scaffolding as needed by year's end.

## Reading: Informational Text (RI.2)
- **RI.2.1** Ask and answer questions about key details.
- **RI.2.2** Identify the main topic of a multiparagraph text and the focus of specific paragraphs.
- **RI.2.3** Describe the connection between historical events, scientific ideas, or steps in a procedure.
- **RI.2.4** Determine the meaning of grade-level words and phrases in a science or social studies text.
- **RI.2.5** Use text features (captions, bold print, subheadings, glossaries, indexes, icons) to find information.
- **RI.2.6** Identify the author's main purpose: what the author wants to answer, explain, or describe.
- **RI.2.7** Explain how images such as a diagram contribute to and clarify a text.
- **RI.2.8** Describe how reasons support the author's specific points.
- **RI.2.9** Compare and contrast the most important points in two texts on the same topic.
- **RI.2.10** Read and comprehend grade 2-3 informational text by year's end.

## Foundational Skills (RF.2)
- **RF.2.3** Know and apply grade-level phonics and word analysis:
  - distinguish long and short vowels in regularly spelled one-syllable words
  - know spelling-sound correspondences for common vowel teams
  - decode regularly spelled two-syllable words with long vowels
  - decode words with common prefixes and suffixes
  - identify words with inconsistent but common spelling-sound patterns
  - recognize grade-appropriate irregularly spelled words
- **RF.2.4** Read with sufficient accuracy and fluency to support comprehension: purpose and understanding, oral reading with accuracy, appropriate rate, and expression on successive readings; use context to confirm or self-correct.

## Writing (W.2)
- **W.2.1** Opinion pieces: introduce the topic, state an opinion, give reasons using linking words (because, and, also), provide a conclusion.
- **W.2.2** Informative/explanatory texts: introduce a topic, use facts and definitions to develop points, provide a conclusion.
- **W.2.3** Narratives: recount a well-elaborated event or short sequence, include actions, thoughts, and feelings, use temporal words, provide closure.
- **W.2.5** With adult guidance, focus on a topic and strengthen writing through revising and editing.
- **W.2.6** With guidance, use digital tools to produce and publish writing, including with peers.
- **W.2.7** Participate in shared research and writing projects.
- **W.2.8** Recall information from experiences or gather it from sources to answer a question.

## Speaking & Listening (SL.2)
- **SL.2.1** Collaborative conversations with peers and adults in small and larger groups: follow agreed-upon rules, build on others' talk, ask for clarification.
- **SL.2.2** Recount or describe key ideas from a text read aloud or information presented orally or through media.
- **SL.2.3** Ask and answer questions about what a speaker says.
- **SL.2.4** Tell a story or recount an experience with relevant facts and descriptive details, speaking audibly in coherent sentences.
- **SL.2.5** Create audio recordings of stories or poems; add drawings or visual displays when appropriate.
- **SL.2.6** Produce complete sentences when appropriate to task and situation.

## Language (L.2)
- **L.2.1** Grammar and usage: collective nouns, frequently occurring irregular plurals, reflexive pronouns, past tense of irregular verbs, adjectives vs. adverbs, produce and rearrange simple and compound sentences.
- **L.2.2** Capitalization, punctuation, and spelling: holidays, product names, geographic names; commas in greetings and closings of letters; apostrophes in contractions and possessives; spelling patterns; consult reference materials.
- **L.2.3** Use knowledge of language and its conventions; compare formal and informal uses of English.
- **L.2.4** Determine meanings of unknown words: sentence context, prefixes, root words, compound words, glossaries and dictionaries.
- **L.2.5** Word relationships: real-life connections, shades of meaning among closely related verbs and adjectives.
- **L.2.6** Use words and phrases acquired through conversations, reading, and being read to.
