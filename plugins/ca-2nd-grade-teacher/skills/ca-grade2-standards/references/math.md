# Grade 2 Math (California Common Core) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

Major emphases for the year: place value to 1,000, fluent addition and subtraction within 100, and measuring length with standard units.

## Operations & Algebraic Thinking (2.OA)
- **2.OA.1** Solve one- and two-step addition and subtraction word problems within 100 across problem types (add to, take from, put together/take apart, compare), with unknowns in any position; represent with drawings and equations using a symbol for the unknown.
- **2.OA.2** Fluently add and subtract within 20 using mental strategies; by year's end, know from memory all sums of two one-digit numbers.
- **2.OA.3** Decide whether a group of up to 20 objects is odd or even (pairing, counting by 2s); write an even number as a sum of two equal addends.
- **2.OA.4** Use addition to find the total in a rectangular array of up to 5 rows and 5 columns; write an equation of equal addends. (Groundwork for multiplication.)

## Number & Operations in Base Ten (2.NBT)
- **2.NBT.1** Three-digit numbers are hundreds, tens, and ones; 100 is a bundle of ten tens; the hundreds 100-900 are 1-9 hundreds with 0 tens and 0 ones.
- **2.NBT.2** Count within 1,000; skip-count by 5s, 10s, and 100s.
- **2.NBT.3** Read and write numbers to 1,000 in numerals, number names, and expanded form.
- **2.NBT.4** Compare two three-digit numbers by place value using >, =, <.
- **2.NBT.5** Fluently add and subtract within 100 using strategies based on place value, properties of operations, and the relationship between addition and subtraction.
- **2.NBT.6** Add up to four two-digit numbers using place-value and property-based strategies.
- **2.NBT.7** Add and subtract within 1,000 using concrete models, drawings, and strategies; relate the strategy to a written method; understand composing and decomposing tens and hundreds.
- **2.NBT.8** Mentally add or subtract 10 or 100 to/from a number between 100 and 900.
- **2.NBT.9** Explain why addition and subtraction strategies work, using place value and properties.

## Measurement & Data (2.MD)
- **2.MD.1** Measure length with rulers, yardsticks, meter sticks, and measuring tapes.
- **2.MD.2** Measure an object twice with different units; explain how the measurements relate to unit size.
- **2.MD.3** Estimate lengths in inches, feet, centimeters, and meters.
- **2.MD.4** Measure how much longer one object is than another, in a standard unit.
- **2.MD.5** Solve length word problems within 100 with addition and subtraction.
- **2.MD.6** Represent whole numbers as lengths on a number line; show sums and differences within 100 there.
- **2.MD.7** Tell and write time from analog and digital clocks to the nearest five minutes, using a.m. and p.m. (California adds knowing the relationships among time units, such as minutes in an hour.)
- **2.MD.8** Solve word problems with dollar bills, quarters, dimes, nickels, and pennies, using $ and ¢ correctly.
- **2.MD.9** Measure several objects to the nearest whole unit and show the data on a line plot.
- **2.MD.10** Draw picture and bar graphs (single-unit scale) for data with up to four categories; solve put-together, take-apart, and compare problems from the graph.

## Geometry (2.G)
- **2.G.1** Recognize and draw shapes with given attributes (number of angles or equal faces); identify triangles, quadrilaterals, pentagons, hexagons, and cubes.
- **2.G.2** Partition a rectangle into rows and columns of same-size squares and count to find the total.
- **2.G.3** Partition circles and rectangles into two, three, or four equal shares; use halves, thirds, half of, a third of, fourths, quarters; equal shares of identical wholes need not have the same shape.

## Standards for Mathematical Practice
MP1 make sense of problems and persevere · MP2 reason abstractly and quantitatively · MP3 construct arguments and critique reasoning · MP4 model with mathematics · MP5 use tools strategically · MP6 attend to precision · MP7 look for structure · MP8 look for regularity in repeated reasoning
