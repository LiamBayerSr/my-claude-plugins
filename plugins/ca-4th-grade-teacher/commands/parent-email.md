Draft a message to a family for this purpose: $ARGUMENTS

Follow the "Writing to families" and "Student privacy" sections of the `fourth-grade-teaching` skill.

- Use "[Student]" and "[Family name]" placeholders, never real names, unless the teacher already wrote the message with them.
- For a concern: open with something specific and positive, state the concern factually and briefly, give one or two concrete things the family can do at home, and invite a reply or a meeting time.
- For good news: be specific about what the student did and why it matters.
- For class announcements: lead with the date, what to bring, and any cost, then details.
- Plain language, under about 150 words, no jargon or acronyms.

If a language was named (or the teacher asks), add a translated version below the English one and note that a fluent speaker should review it before sending.

Offer a shorter text-message version at the end.
