# California grade 4 standards

Paraphrased summaries for planning. Always cite the code. For exact wording, point the teacher to the official California Department of Education documents at https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/ and do not invent codes; if unsure a code exists, say so.

Load only the file you need:
- `references/math.md` - Operations & Algebraic Thinking, Number & Operations in Base Ten, Number & Operations - Fractions, Measurement & Data, Geometry
- `references/ela.md` - Reading Literature, Reading Informational Text, Foundational Skills, Writing, Speaking & Listening, Language
- `references/science.md` - CA NGSS grade 4 performance expectations
- `references/social-studies.md` - History-Social Science grade 4, "California: A Changing State"
- `references/eld.md` - CA ELD standards structure and proficiency levels

## Alignment rules

- Pick the one or two standards a lesson truly targets; list supporting standards separately.
- Match the verb: a standard about "explain" needs students explaining, not just identifying.
- Math lessons should name the Standards for Mathematical Practice they emphasize (e.g., MP3 construct arguments, MP4 model).
- Integrate ELD: pair every content lesson with an ELD focus (collaborative, interpretive, or productive mode).
- Grade 4 includes CAASPP testing in ELA and math. Build real understanding first; use test-like item types only as occasional practice.
