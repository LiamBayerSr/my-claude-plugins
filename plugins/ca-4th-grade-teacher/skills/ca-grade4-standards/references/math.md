# Grade 4 Math (California Common Core) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

Major emphases for the year: multi-digit multiplication and division, fraction equivalence and operations with like denominators, decimals to hundredths, and angles.

## Operations & Algebraic Thinking (4.OA)
- **4.OA.1** Interpret a multiplication equation as a comparison (35 = 5 × 7 means 35 is 5 times as many as 7).
- **4.OA.2** Multiply or divide to solve word problems involving multiplicative comparison, distinguishing it from additive comparison.
- **4.OA.3** Solve multistep word problems with whole numbers using the four operations, including problems where remainders must be interpreted; represent with equations using a letter for the unknown; assess reasonableness with mental computation and estimation.
- **4.OA.4** Find all factor pairs for a whole number 1-100; recognize a number as a multiple of each of its factors; determine whether a number is prime or composite.
- **4.OA.5** Generate a number or shape pattern that follows a given rule and identify apparent features of the pattern not explicit in the rule.

## Number & Operations in Base Ten (4.NBT) - whole numbers to 1,000,000
- **4.NBT.1** A digit in one place represents ten times what it represents in the place to its right.
- **4.NBT.2** Read and write multi-digit whole numbers using numerals, number names, and expanded form; compare two multi-digit numbers using >, =, <.
- **4.NBT.3** Round multi-digit whole numbers to any place.
- **4.NBT.4** Fluently add and subtract multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm.
- **4.NBT.5** Multiply a whole number of up to four digits by a one-digit number, and two two-digit numbers, using place-value strategies; illustrate and explain with equations, arrays, and area models.
- **4.NBT.6** Find whole-number quotients and remainders with up to four-digit dividends and one-digit divisors, using place-value strategies; illustrate and explain with equations, arrays, and area models.

## Number & Operations - Fractions (4.NF) - denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 100
- **4.NF.1** Explain why a fraction a/b is equivalent to (n × a)/(n × b) using visual fraction models; generate equivalent fractions.
- **4.NF.2** Compare two fractions with different numerators and different denominators (common denominators or numerators, or comparing to a benchmark like 1/2); record with >, =, <.
- **4.NF.3** Understand a fraction a/b with a > 1 as a sum of fractions 1/b: add and subtract fractions with like denominators; decompose a fraction into a sum of fractions with the same denominator in more than one way; add and subtract mixed numbers with like denominators; solve word problems.
- **4.NF.4** Multiply a fraction by a whole number: understand a/b as a multiple of 1/b; understand a multiple of a/b as a multiple of 1/b; solve word problems.
- **4.NF.5** Express a fraction with denominator 10 as an equivalent fraction with denominator 100, and add two fractions with denominators 10 and 100.
- **4.NF.6** Use decimal notation for fractions with denominators 10 or 100 (0.62 = 62/100); locate them on a number line.
- **4.NF.7** Compare two decimals to hundredths by reasoning about their size, recognizing comparisons are valid only when they refer to the same whole.

## Measurement & Data (4.MD)
- **4.MD.1** Know relative sizes of measurement units within one system (km, m, cm; kg, g; lb, oz; l, ml; hr, min, sec); express measurements in a larger unit in terms of a smaller unit; record equivalents in a two-column table.
- **4.MD.2** Use the four operations to solve word problems involving distances, intervals of time, liquid volumes, masses of objects, and money, including simple fractions or decimals.
- **4.MD.3** Apply the area and perimeter formulas for rectangles in real-world and mathematical problems.
- **4.MD.4** Make a line plot to display measurements in fractions of a unit (1/2, 1/4, 1/8); solve addition and subtraction problems using the line plot.
- **4.MD.5-6** Understand angles as geometric shapes formed by two rays sharing an endpoint, and angle measurement in degrees relative to a circle; measure angles in whole-number degrees with a protractor and sketch angles of a given measure.
- **4.MD.7** Recognize angle measure as additive; solve addition and subtraction problems to find unknown angles.

## Geometry (4.G)
- **4.G.1** Draw points, lines, line segments, rays, angles (right, acute, obtuse), and perpendicular and parallel lines; identify them in two-dimensional figures.
- **4.G.2** Classify two-dimensional figures based on parallel or perpendicular lines or angles of a specified size; recognize and identify right triangles.
- **4.G.3** Recognize a line of symmetry for a two-dimensional figure; identify line-symmetric figures and draw lines of symmetry.

## Standards for Mathematical Practice
MP1 make sense of problems and persevere · MP2 reason abstractly and quantitatively · MP3 construct arguments and critique reasoning · MP4 model with mathematics · MP5 use tools strategically · MP6 attend to precision · MP7 look for structure · MP8 look for regularity in repeated reasoning
