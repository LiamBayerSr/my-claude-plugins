# Grade 3 English Language Arts (California Common Core) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

## Reading: Literature (RL.3)
- **RL.3.1** Ask and answer questions to demonstrate understanding, referring explicitly to the text as the basis for answers.
- **RL.3.2** Recount stories, including fables, folktales, and myths from diverse cultures; determine the central message, lesson, or moral and explain how it is conveyed through key details.
- **RL.3.3** Describe characters (traits, motivations, feelings) and explain how their actions contribute to the sequence of events.
- **RL.3.4** Determine the meaning of words and phrases as used in a text, distinguishing literal from nonliteral language.
- **RL.3.5** Refer to parts of stories, dramas, and poems using terms such as chapter, scene, and stanza; describe how each part builds on earlier sections.
- **RL.3.6** Distinguish their own point of view from that of the narrator or characters.
- **RL.3.7** Explain how illustrations contribute to what is conveyed by the words (mood, emphasis on aspects of a character or setting).
- **RL.3.9** Compare and contrast themes, settings, and plots of stories by the same author about the same or similar characters.
- **RL.3.10** By year's end, read and comprehend grade 2-3 literature independently and proficiently.

## Reading: Informational Text (RI.3)
- **RI.3.1** Ask and answer questions, referring explicitly to the text.
- **RI.3.2** Determine the main idea; recount the key details and explain how they support the main idea.
- **RI.3.3** Describe the relationship between historical events, scientific ideas, or steps in a procedure, using language about time, sequence, and cause and effect.
- **RI.3.4** Determine the meaning of general academic and domain-specific words and phrases.
- **RI.3.5** Use text features and search tools (key words, sidebars, hyperlinks) to locate information efficiently.
- **RI.3.6** Distinguish their own point of view from that of the author.
- **RI.3.7** Use information from illustrations (maps, photographs) and words to demonstrate understanding.
- **RI.3.8** Describe the logical connection between sentences and paragraphs (comparison, cause/effect, first/second/third in a sequence).
- **RI.3.9** Compare and contrast the most important points and key details in two texts on the same topic.
- **RI.3.10** By year's end, read and comprehend grade 2-3 informational texts independently and proficiently.

## Foundational Skills (RF.3)
- **RF.3.3** Phonics and word analysis: identify and know the meaning of common prefixes and derivational suffixes; decode words with common Latin suffixes; decode multisyllable words; read grade-appropriate irregularly spelled words.
- **RF.3.4** Read with sufficient accuracy and fluency to support comprehension, including oral reading with accuracy, appropriate rate, and expression on successive readings; use context to confirm or self-correct.

## Writing (W.3)
- **W.3.1** Opinion pieces: introduce the topic, state an opinion, create an organizational structure listing reasons, provide reasons that support the opinion, use linking words (because, therefore, since, for example), and provide a concluding statement.
- **W.3.2** Informative texts: introduce a topic and group related information, include illustrations when useful, develop with facts, definitions, and details, use linking words (also, another, and, more, but), and provide a conclusion.
- **W.3.3** Narratives: establish a situation and characters, organize an event sequence, use dialogue and descriptions of actions, thoughts, and feelings, use temporal words, and provide closure.
- **W.3.4-8** With guidance, produce writing with development and organization appropriate to task and purpose; plan, revise, and edit; use technology to produce and publish (including keyboarding skills); conduct short research projects; recall information and gather it from print and digital sources, taking brief notes and sorting evidence into categories.
- **W.3.10** Write routinely over extended and shorter time frames.

## Speaking & Listening (SL.3)
- **SL.3.1** Collaborative discussions: come prepared, follow agreed-upon rules, ask questions to check understanding, stay on topic, link comments to others' remarks, and explain their own ideas in light of the discussion.
- **SL.3.2-3** Determine the main ideas and supporting details of a text read aloud or presented in diverse media; ask and answer questions about a speaker's information, with elaboration and detail.
- **SL.3.4-6** Report on a topic or text, tell a story, or recount an experience with appropriate facts and details, speaking clearly at an understandable pace; create engaging audio recordings; speak in complete sentences when appropriate.

## Language (L.3)
- **L.3.1** Grammar: explain the function of nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs; regular and irregular plural nouns; abstract nouns; regular and irregular verbs; simple verb tenses; subject-verb and pronoun-antecedent agreement; comparative and superlative adjectives and adverbs; coordinating and subordinating conjunctions; simple, compound, and complex sentences. (California adds writing legibly in cursive.)
- **L.3.2** Conventions: capitalize appropriate words in titles; commas in addresses and dialogue; quotation marks in dialogue; possessives; conventional spelling for high-frequency words and adding suffixes; use spelling patterns and generalizations; consult reference materials.
- **L.3.3** Choose words and phrases for effect; recognize and observe differences between spoken and written standard English.
- **L.3.4-6** Determine meanings using context, affixes, and root words; use glossaries and dictionaries; distinguish literal and nonliteral meanings; identify real-life connections; distinguish shades of meaning; acquire and use grade-appropriate academic and domain-specific words.
