# Grade 3 Math (California Common Core) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

Major emphases for the year: multiplication and division within 100, fractions as numbers (especially unit fractions), area, and two-dimensional shapes.

## Operations & Algebraic Thinking (3.OA)
- **3.OA.1** Interpret products of whole numbers as the total in equal groups (5 × 7 is 5 groups of 7).
- **3.OA.2** Interpret whole-number quotients as the number in each share or the number of shares (56 ÷ 8).
- **3.OA.3** Use multiplication and division within 100 to solve word problems involving equal groups, arrays, and measurement quantities, using drawings and equations with a symbol for the unknown.
- **3.OA.4** Determine the unknown whole number in a multiplication or division equation (8 × ? = 48).
- **3.OA.5** Apply properties of operations (commutative, associative, distributive) as strategies to multiply and divide.
- **3.OA.6** Understand division as an unknown-factor problem (find 32 ÷ 8 by finding the number that makes 32 when multiplied by 8).
- **3.OA.7** Fluently multiply and divide within 100 using strategies; by year's end, know from memory all products of two one-digit numbers.
- **3.OA.8** Solve two-step word problems using the four operations; represent them with equations using a letter for the unknown; assess reasonableness using mental computation and estimation, including rounding.
- **3.OA.9** Identify arithmetic patterns (including in the addition and multiplication tables) and explain them using properties of operations.

## Number & Operations in Base Ten (3.NBT)
- **3.NBT.1** Use place value to round whole numbers to the nearest 10 or 100.
- **3.NBT.2** Fluently add and subtract within 1,000 using strategies and algorithms based on place value, properties, and the addition-subtraction relationship.
- **3.NBT.3** Multiply one-digit whole numbers by multiples of 10 in the range 10-90 (9 × 80), using place value and properties.

## Number & Operations - Fractions (3.NF) - denominators 2, 3, 4, 6, 8
- **3.NF.1** Understand a fraction 1/b as one part when a whole is partitioned into b equal parts; understand a/b as a parts of size 1/b.
- **3.NF.2** Understand a fraction as a number on the number line; represent fractions on a number line diagram.
- **3.NF.3** Explain equivalence of fractions and compare fractions by reasoning about their size: recognize and generate simple equivalent fractions; express whole numbers as fractions (3 = 3/1, 4/4 = 1); compare two fractions with the same numerator or the same denominator, recognizing comparisons are valid only when referring to the same whole.

## Measurement & Data (3.MD)
- **3.MD.1** Tell and write time to the nearest minute and measure time intervals in minutes; solve word problems involving addition and subtraction of time intervals (elapsed time).
- **3.MD.2** Measure and estimate liquid volumes and masses using grams, kilograms, and liters; solve one-step word problems involving these.
- **3.MD.3** Draw scaled picture graphs and scaled bar graphs for data with several categories; solve one- and two-step "how many more/less" problems from them.
- **3.MD.4** Measure lengths to the nearest half and quarter inch; show the data on a line plot with whole numbers, halves, or quarters.
- **3.MD.5-6** Understand area as an attribute of plane figures, measured with unit squares; measure areas by counting unit squares (square cm, square m, square in, square ft, and improvised units).
- **3.MD.7** Relate area to multiplication and addition: tile rectangles and show the area equals side lengths multiplied; use area models to represent the distributive property; find areas of rectilinear figures by decomposing them into non-overlapping rectangles.
- **3.MD.8** Solve problems involving perimeters of polygons, including finding an unknown side length and exhibiting rectangles with the same perimeter and different areas or the same area and different perimeters.

## Geometry (3.G)
- **3.G.1** Understand that shapes in different categories may share attributes and that shared attributes can define a larger category (quadrilaterals); recognize rhombuses, rectangles, and squares as quadrilaterals, and draw quadrilaterals that do not belong to these subcategories.
- **3.G.2** Partition shapes into parts with equal areas and express each part's area as a unit fraction of the whole.

## Standards for Mathematical Practice
MP1 make sense of problems and persevere · MP2 reason abstractly and quantitatively · MP3 construct arguments and critique reasoning · MP4 model with mathematics · MP5 use tools strategically · MP6 attend to precision · MP7 look for structure · MP8 look for regularity in repeated reasoning
