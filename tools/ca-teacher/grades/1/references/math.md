# Grade 1 Math (California Common Core) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

Major emphases for the year: addition and subtraction within 20, place value (tens and ones), and measuring length with non-standard units.

## Operations & Algebraic Thinking (1.OA)
- **1.OA.1** Solve addition and subtraction word problems within 20 across problem types (add to, take from, put together, take apart, compare), with unknowns in all positions, using objects, drawings, and equations with a symbol for the unknown.
- **1.OA.2** Solve word problems that call for adding three whole numbers whose sum is 20 or less.
- **1.OA.3** Apply properties of operations as strategies (if 8 + 3 = 11, then 3 + 8 = 11; to add 2 + 6 + 4, make 10 first).
- **1.OA.4** Understand subtraction as an unknown-addend problem (find 10 - 8 by finding what makes 10 with 8).
- **1.OA.5** Relate counting to addition and subtraction (counting on 2 to add 2).
- **1.OA.6** Add and subtract within 20, fluently within 10, using strategies such as counting on, making ten, decomposing to a ten, using the addition-subtraction relationship, and known equivalent sums.
- **1.OA.7** Understand the meaning of the equal sign; decide whether equations are true or false (6 = 6, 5 + 2 = 2 + 5).
- **1.OA.8** Find the unknown whole number in an addition or subtraction equation (8 + ? = 11).

## Number & Operations in Base Ten (1.NBT)
- **1.NBT.1** Count to 120 starting at any number; read and write numerals and represent a number of objects with a numeral in that range.
- **1.NBT.2** The two digits of a two-digit number are tens and ones: 10 is a bundle of ten ones; 11-19 are a ten and some ones; 10, 20, ... 90 are 1-9 tens and 0 ones.
- **1.NBT.3** Compare two two-digit numbers by tens and ones using >, =, <.
- **1.NBT.4** Add within 100, including a two-digit and a one-digit number and a two-digit number and a multiple of 10, using models, drawings, and place-value strategies; understand that sometimes ten ones make a ten.
- **1.NBT.5** Given a two-digit number, mentally find 10 more or 10 less and explain the reasoning.
- **1.NBT.6** Subtract multiples of 10 in the range 10-90 from multiples of 10, using models and strategies.

## Measurement & Data (1.MD)
- **1.MD.1** Order three objects by length; compare the lengths of two objects indirectly by using a third object.
- **1.MD.2** Express the length of an object as a whole number of same-size length units laid end to end with no gaps or overlaps.
- **1.MD.3** Tell and write time in hours and half-hours using analog and digital clocks. (California adds relating time to events, such as before/after and shorter/longer.)
- **1.MD.4** Organize, represent, and interpret data with up to three categories; ask and answer questions about the totals and how many more or less are in one category.

## Geometry (1.G)
- **1.G.1** Distinguish defining attributes (triangles are closed and three-sided) from non-defining attributes (color, orientation, size); build and draw shapes with given attributes.
- **1.G.2** Compose 2D shapes (rectangles, squares, trapezoids, triangles, half-circles, quarter-circles) or 3D shapes (cubes, prisms, cones, cylinders) to create a composite shape, and compose new shapes from it.
- **1.G.3** Partition circles and rectangles into two and four equal shares; use the words halves, fourths, quarters, half of, fourth of, quarter of; understand that more equal shares make smaller shares.

## Standards for Mathematical Practice
MP1 make sense of problems and persevere · MP2 reason abstractly and quantitatively · MP3 construct arguments and critique reasoning · MP4 model with mathematics · MP5 use tools strategically · MP6 attend to precision · MP7 look for structure · MP8 look for regularity in repeated reasoning
