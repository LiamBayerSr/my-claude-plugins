# Grade 1 English Language Arts (California Common Core) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

## Reading: Literature (RL.1)
- **RL.1.1** Ask and answer questions about key details.
- **RL.1.2** Retell stories, including key details, and show understanding of their central message or lesson.
- **RL.1.3** Describe characters, settings, and major events using key details.
- **RL.1.4** Identify words and phrases that suggest feelings or appeal to the senses.
- **RL.1.5** Explain major differences between books that tell stories and books that give information.
- **RL.1.6** Identify who is telling the story at various points.
- **RL.1.7** Use illustrations and details to describe characters, setting, or events.
- **RL.1.9** Compare and contrast the adventures and experiences of characters in stories.
- **RL.1.10** With prompting and support, read prose and poetry of appropriate complexity for grade 1.

## Reading: Informational Text (RI.1)
- **RI.1.1-3** Ask and answer questions about key details; identify the main topic and retell key details; describe the connection between two individuals, events, ideas, or pieces of information.
- **RI.1.4** Ask and answer questions to determine or clarify the meaning of words and phrases.
- **RI.1.5** Know and use text features (headings, tables of contents, glossaries, electronic menus, icons) to locate information.
- **RI.1.6** Distinguish between information from pictures or illustrations and information from the words.
- **RI.1.7-9** Use illustrations and details to describe key ideas; identify reasons an author gives to support points; identify similarities and differences between two texts on the same topic.
- **RI.1.10** With prompting and support, read informational texts of appropriate complexity for grade 1.

## Foundational Skills (RF.1)
- **RF.1.1** Print concepts: recognize the distinguishing features of a sentence (first word, capitalization, ending punctuation).
- **RF.1.2** Phonological awareness: distinguish long from short vowel sounds in one-syllable words; orally blend sounds, including consonant blends, to make words; isolate and pronounce initial, medial vowel, and final sounds; segment one-syllable words into their individual sounds.
- **RF.1.3** Phonics: know spelling-sound correspondences for common consonant digraphs; decode regularly spelled one-syllable words; know final -e and common vowel team conventions for long vowels; use the number of vowel sounds to find syllables; decode two-syllable words by breaking them into syllables; read words with inflectional endings; recognize grade-appropriate irregularly spelled words.
- **RF.1.4** Read with sufficient accuracy and fluency to support comprehension: purpose and understanding, oral reading with accuracy, rate, and expression; use context to confirm or self-correct.

## Writing (W.1)
- **W.1.1** Opinion pieces: introduce the topic or book, state an opinion, give a reason, and provide some closure.
- **W.1.2** Informative texts: name a topic, supply some facts, and provide some closure.
- **W.1.3** Narratives: recount two or more sequenced events with details, use temporal words, and provide some closure.
- **W.1.5-8** With guidance and support, focus on a topic, respond to feedback, and add details; use digital tools to publish; participate in shared research; recall information or gather it from sources to answer a question.

## Speaking & Listening (SL.1)
- **SL.1.1** Collaborative conversations: follow agreed-upon rules; build on others' talk by responding to their comments; ask questions to clear up confusion.
- **SL.1.2-3** Ask and answer questions about key details in a text read aloud or presented orally; ask and answer questions about what a speaker says.
- **SL.1.4-6** Describe people, places, things, and events with details, expressing ideas clearly; add drawings or visuals; produce complete sentences when appropriate.

## Language (L.1)
- **L.1.1** Grammar: print all letters; common, proper, and possessive nouns; singular and plural nouns with matching verbs; personal, possessive, and indefinite pronouns; past, present, and future verb tenses; adjectives; common conjunctions; determiners; frequently occurring prepositions; produce and expand simple and compound declarative, interrogative, imperative, and exclamatory sentences.
- **L.1.2** Conventions: capitalize dates and names of people; end punctuation; commas in dates and to separate words in a series; spell words with common patterns and irregular high-frequency words; spell untaught words phonetically.
- **L.1.4-6** Determine meanings using sentence context, affixes, and root words; sort words into categories; identify real-life connections; distinguish shades of meaning among verbs and adjectives; use words acquired through conversations, reading, and being read to.
