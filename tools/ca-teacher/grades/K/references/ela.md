# Kindergarten English Language Arts (California Common Core) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

Most kindergarten reading standards are met "with prompting and support" and through read-alouds.

## Reading: Literature (RL.K)
- **RL.K.1** With prompting and support, ask and answer questions about key details.
- **RL.K.2** With prompting and support, retell familiar stories, including key details.
- **RL.K.3** With prompting and support, identify characters, settings, and major events.
- **RL.K.4** Ask and answer questions about unknown words in a text.
- **RL.K.5** Recognize common types of texts (storybooks, poems; California adds fantasy and realistic text).
- **RL.K.6** With prompting and support, name the author and illustrator and define their roles.
- **RL.K.7** With prompting and support, describe how illustrations relate to the story.
- **RL.K.9** With prompting and support, compare and contrast characters' adventures and experiences in familiar stories.
- **RL.K.10** Actively engage in group reading activities with purpose and understanding.

## Reading: Informational Text (RI.K)
- **RI.K.1-3** With prompting and support, ask and answer questions about key details, identify the main topic and retell key details, and describe connections between individuals, events, ideas, or pieces of information.
- **RI.K.4** With prompting and support, ask and answer questions about unknown words.
- **RI.K.5-6** Identify the front cover, back cover, and title page; name the author and illustrator and their roles in presenting ideas.
- **RI.K.7-9** With prompting and support, describe how illustrations relate to the text, identify the reasons an author gives, and compare two texts on the same topic.
- **RI.K.10** Actively engage in group reading of informational texts.

## Foundational Skills (RF.K)
- **RF.K.1** Print concepts: follow words left to right, top to bottom, page by page; know spoken words are represented by print; know words are separated by spaces; recognize and name all upper- and lowercase letters.
- **RF.K.2** Phonological awareness: recognize and produce rhyming words; count, pronounce, blend, and segment syllables; blend and segment onsets and rimes; isolate and pronounce initial, medial vowel, and final sounds in three-phoneme (CVC) words; add or substitute sounds to make new words.
- **RF.K.3** Phonics: know the primary sound for each consonant; associate long and short sounds with the five major vowels; read common high-frequency words by sight; distinguish similarly spelled words by the sounds that differ.
- **RF.K.4** Read emergent-reader texts with purpose and understanding.

## Writing (W.K)
- **W.K.1** Use drawing, dictating, and writing to compose opinion pieces (tell the topic or book and state an opinion).
- **W.K.2** Use drawing, dictating, and writing to compose informative texts (name the topic and supply some information).
- **W.K.3** Use drawing, dictating, and writing to narrate an event or several loosely linked events, in order, with a reaction.
- **W.K.5-8** With guidance and support, respond to questions and suggestions from peers and add details; explore digital tools; participate in shared research; recall information from experience or provided sources.

## Speaking & Listening (SL.K)
- **SL.K.1** Participate in collaborative conversations: follow agreed-upon rules (listening, taking turns); continue a conversation through multiple exchanges.
- **SL.K.2-3** Confirm understanding of a text read aloud by asking and answering questions; ask and answer questions to get help or clarify.
- **SL.K.4-6** Describe familiar people, places, things, and events with details; add drawings or visuals; speak audibly and express thoughts, feelings, and ideas clearly.

## Language (L.K)
- **L.K.1** Grammar: print many letters; use frequent nouns and verbs; form regular plurals; use question words; use common prepositions; produce and expand complete sentences in shared activities.
- **L.K.2** Conventions: capitalize the first word in a sentence and the pronoun I; recognize and name end punctuation; write a letter for most consonant and short-vowel sounds; spell simple words phonetically.
- **L.K.4-6** Determine meanings of new words (including multiple-meaning words and common inflections); sort objects into categories; relate words to opposites; use words acquired through conversations, reading, and being read to.
