# Grade 2 Science (California NGSS) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/pd/ca/sc/ngssstandards.asp

CA NGSS is three-dimensional: each performance expectation (PE) combines a science or engineering practice, a disciplinary core idea, and a crosscutting concept. Lessons should have students doing the practice (investigating, modeling, arguing from evidence), not just learning facts.

## Physical Science - Structure and Properties of Matter
- **2-PS1-1** Plan and carry out an investigation to describe and classify materials by observable properties (color, texture, hardness, flexibility).
- **2-PS1-2** Analyze test data to decide which materials have the properties best suited to a purpose.
- **2-PS1-3** Use evidence to explain how an object made of small pieces can be taken apart and made into a new object.
- **2-PS1-4** Argue from evidence that heating or cooling causes some changes that can be reversed (melting ice) and some that cannot (cooking an egg).

## Life Science - Interdependent Relationships in Ecosystems
- **2-LS2-1** Plan and conduct an investigation into whether plants need sunlight and water to grow.
- **2-LS2-2** Develop a simple model that mimics how animals disperse seeds or pollinate plants.
- **2-LS4-1** Observe plants and animals to compare the diversity of life in different habitats.

## Earth & Space Science - Earth's Systems: Processes that Shape the Earth
- **2-ESS1-1** Use information from several sources to show that Earth events can happen quickly (volcanic eruptions, earthquakes) or slowly (erosion).
- **2-ESS2-1** Compare multiple solutions designed to slow or prevent wind or water from changing the shape of the land.
- **2-ESS2-2** Develop a model to represent the shapes and kinds of land and bodies of water in an area.
- **2-ESS2-3** Obtain information to identify where water is found on Earth and that it can be solid or liquid.

## Engineering Design (K-2)
- **K-2-ETS1-1** Ask questions, make observations, and gather information about a situation people want to change, to define a simple problem solvable with a new or improved tool.
- **K-2-ETS1-2** Develop a simple sketch, drawing, or physical model showing how an object's shape helps it solve a problem.
- **K-2-ETS1-3** Analyze data from tests of two objects designed to solve the same problem to compare their strengths and weaknesses.

## California connections
California's Environmental Principles and Concepts are meant to be woven into science and social studies. Grade 2 topics connect naturally: local habitats, erosion along California coasts and rivers, earthquakes, and drought and water sources.
