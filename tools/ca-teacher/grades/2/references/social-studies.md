# Grade 2 History-Social Science (California) - paraphrased

Theme: **People Who Make a Difference.** Students learn about people who supply goods and services, family and community history, and individuals who made a difference.

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

- **2.1** Differentiate between things that happened long ago and things that happened yesterday: trace family history through primary sources (photos, artifacts, documents, interviews), compare daily life of parents and grandparents with today, place family events on a timeline.
- **2.2** Demonstrate map skills: describe the absolute and relative locations of people, places, and environments; label a simple map of the neighborhood, school, and the student's community; locate continents, oceans, major mountain ranges, and the student's state and nation on maps and globes; compare and contrast basic land use in urban, suburban, and rural settings in California.
- **2.3** Explain governmental institutions and practices in the United States and other countries: the purpose of rules and laws, what a good citizen does, and how groups and nations interact and resolve conflicts.
- **2.4** Understand basic economic concepts and their individual roles in the economy: how food goes from farm to table (including California agriculture), producers and consumers, goods and services, how people earn income, why people trade, and the role of scarcity and choice.
- **2.5** Understand the importance of individual action and character, and explain how heroes from long ago and the recent past have made a difference in others' lives (for example, scientists, inventors, authors, civil-rights leaders, and other figures from diverse backgrounds).

## Analysis skills for K-5 (apply across topics)
Chronological thinking (timelines, past/present/future), spatial thinking (maps and geography), and research, evidence, and point of view (primary vs. secondary sources, asking questions about the past).

## Teaching notes
- Family-history work (2.1) must be inclusive: offer options that do not require a two-parent family, known ancestry, or photos at home, and let students choose any caring adult or a community history instead.
- Choose 2.5 heroes that reflect the class's communities and California's diversity.
