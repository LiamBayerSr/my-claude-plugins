# Grade 5 Math (California Common Core) - paraphrased

Official text: https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/

Major emphases for the year: adding and subtracting fractions with unlike denominators, multiplying and dividing fractions, decimal operations to hundredths, and volume.

## Operations & Algebraic Thinking (5.OA)
- **5.OA.1** Use parentheses, brackets, or braces in numerical expressions and evaluate expressions with these symbols.
- **5.OA.2** Write simple expressions that record calculations with numbers, and interpret numerical expressions without evaluating them ("add 8 and 7, then multiply by 2" is 2 × (8 + 7)). (California adds 5.OA.2.1: express a whole number in the range 2-50 as a product of its prime factors.)
- **5.OA.3** Generate two numerical patterns using two given rules; identify relationships between corresponding terms; form ordered pairs and graph them on a coordinate plane.

## Number & Operations in Base Ten (5.NBT)
- **5.NBT.1** In a multi-digit number, a digit in one place represents 10 times as much as it represents in the place to its right and 1/10 of what it represents in the place to its left.
- **5.NBT.2** Explain patterns in the number of zeros of the product when multiplying by powers of 10, and patterns in the placement of the decimal point when multiplying or dividing a decimal by a power of 10; use whole-number exponents to denote powers of 10.
- **5.NBT.3** Read, write, and compare decimals to thousandths (numerals, number names, expanded form; >, =, <).
- **5.NBT.4** Use place value understanding to round decimals to any place.
- **5.NBT.5** Fluently multiply multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm.
- **5.NBT.6** Find whole-number quotients with up to four-digit dividends and two-digit divisors, using place-value strategies; illustrate and explain with equations, arrays, and area models.
- **5.NBT.7** Add, subtract, multiply, and divide decimals to hundredths using concrete models, drawings, and strategies; relate the strategy to a written method and explain the reasoning.

## Number & Operations - Fractions (5.NF)
- **5.NF.1** Add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators (including mixed numbers) by replacing them with equivalent fractions with like denominators.
- **5.NF.2** Solve word problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions referring to the same whole; use benchmark fractions and number sense to estimate and assess reasonableness.
- **5.NF.3** Interpret a fraction as division of the numerator by the denominator (a/b = a ÷ b); solve word problems involving division of whole numbers leading to fraction or mixed-number answers.
- **5.NF.4** Multiply a fraction or whole number by a fraction; find the area of a rectangle with fractional side lengths by tiling with unit squares and show it equals multiplying the side lengths.
- **5.NF.5** Interpret multiplication as scaling (resizing): compare the size of a product to the size of one factor without multiplying; explain why multiplying by a fraction greater than 1 gives a larger product and by a fraction less than 1 gives a smaller one.
- **5.NF.6** Solve real-world problems involving multiplication of fractions and mixed numbers.
- **5.NF.7** Divide unit fractions by whole numbers and whole numbers by unit fractions; solve real-world problems involving these divisions.

## Measurement & Data (5.MD)
- **5.MD.1** Convert among different-sized standard measurement units within a given system (5 cm to 0.05 m) and use the conversions in multistep real-world problems.
- **5.MD.2** Make a line plot to display measurements in fractions of a unit (1/2, 1/4, 1/8) and use fraction operations to solve problems involving the data.
- **5.MD.3-4** Recognize volume as an attribute of solid figures; a cube with side length 1 unit is a unit cube; measure volume by counting unit cubes (cubic cm, cubic in, cubic ft, and improvised units).
- **5.MD.5** Relate volume to multiplication and addition: find the volume of a right rectangular prism by packing it with unit cubes; apply V = l × w × h and V = b × h; recognize volume as additive and find volumes of solid figures made of two non-overlapping prisms.

## Geometry (5.G)
- **5.G.1** Use a pair of perpendicular number lines (axes) to define a coordinate system; understand that the first number indicates distance along the x-axis and the second along the y-axis.
- **5.G.2** Represent real-world and mathematical problems by graphing points in the first quadrant and interpret coordinate values in context.
- **5.G.3** Understand that attributes belonging to a category of two-dimensional figures also belong to all subcategories (all rectangles have four right angles, and squares are rectangles).
- **5.G.4** Classify two-dimensional figures in a hierarchy based on properties.

## Standards for Mathematical Practice
MP1 make sense of problems and persevere · MP2 reason abstractly and quantitatively · MP3 construct arguments and critique reasoning · MP4 model with mathematics · MP5 use tools strategically · MP6 attend to precision · MP7 look for structure · MP8 look for regularity in repeated reasoning
